ℹ️ About This App

Lanka Lottery Checker scans the QR/barcode on National Lotteries Board (NLB) and Development Lotteries Board (DLB) tickets, identifies the lottery & draw number, fetches the live official result, and compares it to your ticket.

Why enter numbers manually? The QR code only proves a ticket's draw and serial — it does not contain your chosen numbers (only NLB/DLB's private servers hold that). So after scanning, just tap in the numbers printed on your ticket to compare.

Results are fetched live from nlb.lk and dlb.lk. This is an independent checking tool and is not affiliated with NLB or DLB. Always confirm with the official board before redeeming a prize.
